01

Quantum-Inspired PSO (QPSO)

A quantum-inspired, classical metaheuristic: the Sun, Feng & Xu delta-potential-well position update with mean-best (mbest) attraction, plus local search and heuristic seeding for CVRPTW routing. No quantum hardware is involved.

02

Empirical Benchmarking (GA, ACO, SA)

Side-by-side comparison with Genetic Algorithms (OX crossover), Ant Colony pheromones, Simulated Annealing, and the Clarke-Wright savings heuristic, under the same evaluation budget with 10 seeded runs each.

03

BPR Congestion & Fleet Types

Travel times grow non-linearly with road load using the Bureau of Public Roads (BPR) volume-over-capacity function, or come from live TomTom traffic. Fleet runs compare EV, cargo and bike profiles and report energy use and estimated CO₂.
