What the optimizer balances

The fleet optimiser minimises one cost: total distance, plus 0.4 × total route time (including traffic delay from the BPR congestion model or live TomTom times), plus heavy penalties for capacity overload, late arrivals and using more vehicles than the fleet has. QPSO searches for the set of routes with the lowest cost. The code is in src/vrp-engine.js and src/vrp-extras.js. Run npm test to check it.

Judge suggestion: heavy traffic ahead

Open Plan a Trip, choose a route, and look under the route list. The alert shows the distance ahead, the extra minutes, when it usually clears, and a real place to stop. The driver picks Keep going or Wait nearby and leave at the clear time.
